- **Restart recovery handles worktree settings and deleted symlinked transcripts, and refuses more ambiguous writers ([#1115](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/1115)).** A worktree child whose session directory differs from the parent's settings can be resumed or steered after a restart, and earlier IDs keep resolving to the newest one after its transcript is deleted when its rows journaled the canonical path. A transcript written in the last 120 seconds is refused as possibly running whatever its last journaled status, and one dated in the future is refused too, with the skew and a finite wait in the refusal instead of "retry in 120 s" forever. A reopen is bound to the transcript its row journaled, so a retargeted link cannot redirect it. A failing journal write no longer fails the child run.
