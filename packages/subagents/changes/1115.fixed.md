- **Restart recovery handles worktree settings, clock skew and deleted symlinked transcripts ([#1115](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/1115)).** A worktree child whose session directory differs from the parent's settings can be resumed or steered after a restart, a transcript dated in the future is still refused as possibly running but its refusal names the skew and a finite wait instead of counting down from 120 s forever, and earlier IDs keep resolving to the newest one after its transcript is deleted. A failing journal write no longer fails the child run.
