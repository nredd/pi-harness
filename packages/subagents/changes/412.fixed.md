- **Token counts just under a million read `1.0M`, not `1000.0k` ([#412](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/412), [#491](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/pull/491)).** The widget, FleetView rows and the workflow card and dialog picked the k/M unit before rounding, so 999,950-999,999 tokens showed `1000.0k`. They now share one `formatCompactTokens` that rounds first; every other count reads as before.
