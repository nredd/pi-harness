- **Subagent transcripts have one writer at a time ([#1247](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/1247)).** Every run that writes a persisted child transcript holds an exclusive `<transcript>.writer` claim until it settles, and a restart reopen takes it before rechecking and opening the file. A transcript another live process writes is refused as in use, even when that process has been idle past the 120-second write window, and a startup lookup reports it as possibly running. A crashed writer's claim is taken over only when its pid is verifiably gone on this host; another host's claim, a live or reused pid, an unreadable claim or an interrupted takeover refuses and names the file to remove. A reopen refuses a transcript replaced or redirected while it is opened.
