- **Deadline-bounded repeating schedules and reusable one-shot names ([#850](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/850)).** `schedule` accepts ` until <ISO or +2h>` on a cron or interval, so a poller stops at its deadline without the parent re-arming it. A fired one-shot or expired job releases its name for reuse. Two pi processes on one session never run the same job at once, and a crashed owner frees its name within eight minutes.
