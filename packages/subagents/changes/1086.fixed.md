- **Subagents can reopen after a process restart ([#1086](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/1086)).** Explicit resume or steer continues a saved child transcript under a new ID, without starting any work on startup. Recovery verifies child ownership, supports children launched in another working directory, and tells you how long to wait before retrying a recently active child. Unknown IDs and refused resumes now return tool errors.
