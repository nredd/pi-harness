- **Subagent transcripts survive tmpfs and `systemd-tmpfiles` ([#900](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/900)).** The temp root remains the fallback (unwritable dir, or `ENOSPC`/`EROFS` on the first write) and is refused unless it is a real directory you own. Session dirs idle for 30 days are pruned shortly after session start, capped at 1000 per start, and dirs the session still references are kept. A transcript an eviction note points at can be pruned once idle; the note keeps its capped result text in memory. Detached-result spool files stay in the temp root.
