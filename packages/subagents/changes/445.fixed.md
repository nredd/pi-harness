- **Cancel stale scheduled admissions ([#445](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/445)).** Disabling or updating a job, stopping the scheduler, or restarting its session cancels pending admission timers and listeners; repeating start also cancels previously armed timers. A cancelled pending admission restores the job's previous status instead of leaving it `running`. Already spawned agents are not stopped. This does not establish the cause of the WSL2 guest stall.
