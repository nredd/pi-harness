- **Two pi processes never double-fire a repeating schedule ([#914](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/914)).** A fire now claims a slot under the store lock: interval slots are `createdAt + k * interval` (timers aim at that shared grid), a cron's is the tick it was armed for, in the process's local timezone (processes with different `TZ` on one session can still double-fire a cron job), a one-shot's its target. Cron ticks are now armed with the injected clock instead of croner's own timer. The job persists `lastSlot` and a claim must be for a later one, so N intervals fire exactly N times across processes. A timer armed before another process edited or re-parked the job is refused and no longer disables the new one-shot schedule. A live pid already outranks a lapsed lease for three lease periods, so a host sleep does not orphan a run.
