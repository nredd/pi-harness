- Record each outbox's publication watermark in `outboxes/<sha256>.published.json` before every usage export publishes, and bound the next `exportedAt` by the later of it and the readable manifest, so an export over a corrupt manifest (or, under `--replace-harnesses`, a denied or missing one) or a restored older manifest still publishes after the last export and viewers holding it import the new one; a missing, unreadable or invalid watermark is ignored, failing to write it fails the export before anything is published, and the `no readable published manifest bounds this export` warning now fires only when the watermark is lost too, e.g. deleted, rekeyed by a moved checkout, or never recorded by an older kit ([#1325](https://github.com/nredd/pi-harness/issues/1325)).
